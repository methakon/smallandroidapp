## Before it may be used in Google Play

This video shows animated **design mockups**, **not a recording of versionCode 9**. Visually inspect the complete rendered MP4 before using it. Ideally substitute real on-device video of the shipped app so no frame misrepresents actual features or screen state. Do not claim ultrasound, health effects, guaranteed hearing safety, insect/animal deterrence, overnight playback or unavailable pink/white noise.

The Play Console's optional preview-video field needs a **public/unlisted YouTube URL**, not the MP4 itself. Upload to YouTube through an authorized account only after checking content, permissions and applicable policy. Video creation is **not** a blocker for the required Play store icon, feature graphic, screenshots or privacy policy.
