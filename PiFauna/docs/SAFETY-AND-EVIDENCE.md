# Safety, animal welfare and claims policy

## Claim policy

**Allowed**: "generates a short digital test signal", "offers illustrative research bands", "stores manual observations", "built-in speaker playback attempted".

**Not allowed without rigorous verification**: "repels mosquitoes", "prevents malaria/dengue", "eliminates rats", "guarantees 10 metre coverage", "safe/inaudible for humans", "harmless to pets", "produces 40–70 kHz ultrasound", "clinically proven".

Do not present insect, rodent, dog, cat or bird frequencies as experimentally validated repellency presets. Species detect frequencies differently; hearing sensitivity is not evidence of avoidance or long-term effectiveness.

## Operating warnings

- Keep media volume low; never use headphones. Do not target people, children, pets, wildlife or neighbors.
- Short experiments only. Stop at any sign of discomfort, distress or unexpected behavior.
- Never use sound to punish, frighten, herd or forcibly exclude an animal. Do not attempt continuous exposure.
- Airborne sounds in the audible band can attract certain animals, even when avoidance is expected.
- Ultrasonic repellents have a weak evidence base; prefer physical exclusion, sanitation, standing-water removal and evidence-based pest management.
- Do not use PiFauna as a substitute for mosquito netting, approved repellents or professional pest control.

## Ethical experimental method

- Observe passively when possible and avoid exposing animals to aversive sound.
- Record **no change**, environmental confounds and whether an animal was actually present.
- Do not conduct prolonged repeated trials just to obtain a desired response; stop if any distress is noticed.
- Use silent control conditions and independent outcome scoring before making any causal claim.
