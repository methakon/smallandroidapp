OFFLINE AUDIO EXPERIMENT

Mindful listening, with clear boundaries

Layaswarna is an experimental Android sound generator. It offers adjustable audible tones, example animal-related frequency bands, symbolic chakra tone associations, and short, user-controlled listening sessions. It is not medical care, a sleep treatment, or a proven animal or pest repellent.

Privacy-first by design

The current Android app does not request network, location, microphone, or storage permissions and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. Settings and named presets are saved on the device. The app is distributed through Google Play, whose own services have separate data practices.

Please read before use. A digital frequency or volume setting is not proof of the sound pressure or frequency produced by your particular phone. Stop playback immediately if discomfort is experienced by any person or animal.
